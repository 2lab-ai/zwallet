Cause addressed: a parallel fork can inherit fs::copy's writable descriptor. Closing
it in the test thread does not close the inherited copy before that child's exec.
The exact fork was not identified by the CI trace; this is the known mechanism at
https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/114554 and the harness has parallel pre_exec
PTY/fd-inheritance children. O_CLOEXEC is too late to prevent the transient ETXTBSY.

Fix: the three executable-copy fixtures now use a shared helper. It locks the new
writer, copies bytes and permissions, closes that writer without explicitly unlocking,
and obtains a shared lock on an independent read-only descriptor before returning.
Fork/dup share the writer's open-file description and lock, so the barrier cannot pass
until all inherited writers close. No spawn retry, fixed delay or suite serialization
was added. Existing destination files are refused rather than truncated.
