TUI-1c, the third of three PRs for `zwallet tui` (v0.2 TUI, `.prd/03-tui.md`; split in `.prd/v0-2-tui/units.md` §TUI-1 split). It runs the real `zwallet tui` on a pseudo-terminal, as a human starts it, and checks what reaches the screen. Closes acceptance T9 and T10. No `src` change: the tests pass on TUI-1b's code (#18).

## What these tests prove for users
- **T9, the terminal comes back.**
  - After `q`, the TUI exits with status 0 and nothing on stderr.
  - The terminal is back in canonical mode with echo.
  - On a 79x24 terminal the screen is the one `terminal too small (80x24 needed)` line and nothing else. This holds when the TUI starts at 79x24, and when a loaded 120x40 screen is resized to 79x24.
- **T10, a lock from another shell shows.** `zwallet lock` in a second process makes the bar say `locked` within two refreshes (10 s). Measured 3.1–4.9 s over 22 runs.
- **A fake daemon cannot paint the terminal.** A process started with the daemon's argv, whose status reply carries 600 characters of ESC and block glyphs as its expiry, is drawn escaped, cut to its budget, and on one line only. No raw ESC from it reaches the terminal.
- **Every pty run is checked for stray bytes.** The TUI's whole output, up to leaving the alternate screen, holds no byte outside a known sequence and no unfinished sequence.

## What changed (tests only)
- **`tests/common/mod.rs`**, the pty helpers (decision 5 in units.md):
  - `openpty_sized` and `PtyChild::spawn_full_screen`. The child gets the pty as its controlling terminal. The parent keeps no slave after the spawn, so the reader ends after the last byte on Linux as well as macOS.
  - `PtyChild::resize` sets the pty's window (TIOCSWINSZ); the kernel sends SIGWINCH.
  - `wait_output` reports an exited child as soon as every byte is read, not at the end of the wait.
  - `Frame`: a parser for the stream ratatui writes. It holds an unfinished trailing sequence as pending, and `final_frame` judges the output up to the first `ESC[?1049l`.
  - The terminal modes are read through the master, so `quit()` works on both platforms.
  - The argv-listed attacker and its five helpers moved here from `tests/cli.rs`, unchanged. `tests/cli.rs` now only imports them.
- **`tests/tui.rs`**: the T9 and T10 pty tests, the argv-listed fake, a parser test with a valid stream cut at every byte, the 79x24 last-frame check, and the early-exit test.
- **`.prd`**: the TUI-1c rows, T9/T10 point at their pty runs, flow.md points at the attacker's new home, and the open lines for TUI-2.
