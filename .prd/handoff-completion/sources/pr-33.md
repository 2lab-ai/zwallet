## What changes for users
- **A Tron address from the same seed:** `m/44'/195'/0'/0/i`, base58check `T…`. It is cached as `addresses.tron` and verified by the session after `unlock`, with no prompt. On a terminal, its QR code scans to its exact case.
- **`balance` has a `tron` row**, with TRX and USDT (TRC20, 6 decimals). Sepolia stays last. A token error stays in that token's row.
- **An owner-permission warning** appears when the account's owner permission is not this key (fixture `tests/fixtures/tron-account-taken-over.json`).
- **The node must be mainnet.** A Nile (testnet) genesis is `RPC_ERROR` before any read.
- **Old keys:** `WALLET_LOCKED` while locked. `--verify` adds the missing Tron address.
- **Refusals:**
  - `sign-message`, `call` and `invoke` on `tron` give `CAPABILITY_UNSUPPORTED` (TR24).
  - A case-flipped cache, a bad checksum or a `0x` cache is refused (TR4).
